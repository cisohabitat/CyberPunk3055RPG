# Action-specific scene sounds

Four short procedural effects make existing devices more tangible while retaining every authored word, actual speaker, illustration and earned outcome. The flow is a legal player choice → engine commitment and local save → lazy static WAV request → native playback, completion or cancellation. No backend or runtime synthesis is added.

## Actions and meanings

- **Chapel viewer:** a newly inspected signature, receipt or roster receives muted glass/contact and a short lens motor. Reopening an already inspected source supplies no cue and no new authentication.
- **Archive reader:** actual index queries and issuing-key reader operations receive dry contacts and a low transport motor after the outcome is committed. The same sound accompanies success and failure; observation, a catalog claim and a paper countersignature supply no reader operation. An uncommitted or restored held die remains silent until Continue accepts the recorded outcome.
- **Courier case:** inspecting the intact cold seal receives a soft shell/strap contact. It depicts a closed parcel, with no opened latch, broken seal, handover, receipt or treatment.
- **Hearing recorder:** entering the hearing from the authored consent steps receives a restrained running transport. Leaving Nia’s words private uses the same neutral mechanism. Publication, permission and the source answer remain separate game choices.

The existing memory bench, archive settings, canal/courier art and closer Ives hearing painting remain appropriate. The 197-scene art audit retains every explicit decision and the two deliberate text cards. No scene setting, actor, quotation or visual result changes, so no replacement painting or voice delivery is needed. The retained voice and artwork hashes are checked separately.

## Playback and cancellation

Only the committed action queues a cue. The cue starts after the destination state and sound plan have rendered; initial loading, save restoration and ordinary paragraph reveal do not create it. A latest-action reference is consumed once, and each native request has a cancellation generation so a delayed fetch cannot start in a later scene. Another action, modal, sheet/notebook, mute, zero effects and pagehide cancel current or pending playback. Closing a dialog or Resume sound consumes no new action and starts no old cue. A cancelled download may finish decoding into the existing bounded 24 MiB cache.

The existing Effects slider remains separate from voice volume. Scene cues use gain 0.5, reduced to 35% of that gain while an actor actually speaks; the playing cue also follows speech start/end smoothly. This affects only the four scene effects. Existing dice, memory feedback, music/ambience ducking and voice loudness trims retain their behavior. Sources disconnect and clear buffers on completion or cancellation. Missing files leave committed choices, readable text and saves usable.

## Authoring and provenance

The four mono 22,050 Hz PCM16 WAV files total 194,216 bytes and last approximately 0.76–1.44 seconds. Original seeded synthesis combines filtered noise, low motors and restrained contact transients, with 8 ms onset / 25 ms tail fades. These are procedural development effects, without third-party recordings or an AI music service. `scripts/author-scene-foley.mjs` is optional offline authoring; it measures delivered PCM peak, RMS, sample jumps, durations and hashes. It never runs in game or build.

`qa/media/scene-foley.json` pins the recipe and deliveries and documents neutral meanings and retained art. The media audition and credits accompany it. All previously delivered audio, including the 290 active voices, original 101 WAV quotations, title theme and background deliveries, remains byte-identical. Exact dialogue bindings and voice mastering remain unchanged.
