This increment follows the AAA production program with one complete optional mission and conduct-based character continuity. It does not replace the existing six-phase program or claim independent acceptance.

## Steps and deliverables

1. **Conduct, not a new reputation score.** Derive Quill, Mara and Kerr's player-visible summaries from committed actions and attributed sources. Preserve historical refusals, betrayals and corrections. Add a once-only late repayment of Quill's forty-creds tab; repayment does not erase the earlier refusal.
2. **A personal contract.** Kerr asks for his own unpowered brace from a canal depot. Agree to a private collection, consent to a registered collection, or decline honestly. Quill can supply an introduction; a previously postponed tab requires repayment before he supplies it. The introduction improves negotiation, not every skill check.
3. **Playable planning.** Choose an entry (stairs, ten-creds anonymous lift, registered courier gate) and exit (sensor ramp, patrol gap, maintenance stairs). Save either partial selection. Planning costs nothing; departure charges the lift and physical effort once. A preview names costs and the identity a registered route exposes. Reset before departure without losing the contract. Native choices work with keyboard, touch, controller and text-only mode.
4. **Build identity and recovery.** Chrome uses the sensor ramp, Ghost uses the patrol gap, Nerve uses the stairs, and Face negotiates at the counter using an earned introduction when available. A failed approach leaves the brace held at the depot, with a paid registered courier or unresolved return. Registered entry remains traced even if an exit succeeds privately. Delivering equipment is not receiving treatment and supplies no historical evidence or witness permission.
5. **Delayed relationship consequence.** Kerr responds to fulfilled private terms, agreed registration, broken private terms or an unfinished promise. A broken promise can receive an honest acknowledgement or a denial that closes future contact. Acknowledgement preserves the original trace. Quill's later conversation remembers his introduction and actual repayment. All four original finales include the resulting character continuity.
6. **Art and verification.** Ship one neutral canal dispatch illustration and explicit decisions for every new scene, reusing suitable Quill/Kerr portraits for conversations. Preserve text-only delivery and optional audio. Verify all approach/recovery outcomes, partial planning, save/export/reload, recorded dice, one-time costs and replies, resource bounds, source limits, all existing campaign fixtures, phone/desktop layouts and the exact deployed revision.
