Preparation and submission consume separate depot actions; pause/cancel preserves committed effort and traces. An acknowledged intake remains an intake, and the later personal reply adds no review result or witness permission. The scarce route genuinely reaches zero credits through legal play. This is practical variation beyond an odds bonus, although the surrounding campaign still contains many explanatory tasks.

**People are present, but explanation often closes the beat.** The Dispatcher's “I knew whose table it could come back to. That mattered to me” supplies a personal response. Its following prose and action details return immediately to reward/source/permission limits. Type is legible, portrait identity is retained and the neutral ramp depicts no successful delivery. The enlarged purchase description can occupy almost an entire available phone reading area. The optional Kerr views remain a useful deliberate inspection feature; their default closed state does not change the dramatic composition of most scenes. **Playback and acting remain different questions.** The voiced campaigns complete 122 dialogues with the expected words/actors and cleanup. Phase 14 retains all fourteen candidate recordings and its ASR disagreements; the source-summary stays original after rejected retakes. Source matching, signal headroom and completion support technical confidence.
