## Player flow

1. Retain source custody and the acquired cancellation receipt. From the canal, ask Edda what the recorded sources can change. A tested case provides a named lead; the existing direct source-review route remains available.
2. Deliberately share the receipt alone or attach the actual corroborating ledger. No witness account or address is attached. Revising the current packet never removes something Edda already learned.
3. Choose a signed public recipient-register review or a private service query. The public request records the runner's handle, raises Helion standing/exposure by one and closes the side service hatch during review. The main counter offers registered sorting work. The private query preserves ordinary service access and offers a short rest appointment. Neither supplies a review finding or guaranteed anonymity.
4. Ask Kerr for an introduction. Share the same source summary and any broken private-collection docket, or keep the case private and discuss only the depot desk. An undisclosed docket gives him no knowledge unless he observed the earlier handover or already reviewed that collection. Once told, he cannot forget it. Acknowledgement can open this limited introduction; denial closes it. Earlier recipient registration and contact denial remain.
5. Allocate two saved depot actions. Reading, conversation, inspecting sources, voices, settings, accessibility controls and pausing spend none. There is no clock tied to reading speed.
6. Return to Edda in The Wall to hear the actual local result. Her archive shift, payroll application and records inquiry retain their separate existing outcomes.

## Character and save boundaries

`districtActorKnowledge` separates firsthand experience, deliberate disclosure and response. The collapsible depot ledger is written interface history, not a new claim that an actor speaks or endorses the narrator. It grants no evidence or permission.

District flags live in the existing bounded flag map. No save version/key migration or required new extension is introduced. The action-window helper follows the existing Chapel pattern: two, one or zero, with the smallest conflicting imported allowance prevailing. Actual legal engine choices spend it; no render, audio callback or timer changes progress. Unstarted runs preserve their state; earlier complete campaign state/prose/dialogue hashes remain checked.
