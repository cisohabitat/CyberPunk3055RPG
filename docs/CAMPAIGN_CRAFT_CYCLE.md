# Campaign craft cycle

## Finding and scope

The preceding audio cycle left campaign pacing and optional-work density as the next delivery. A paired source audit of 108 complete authored campaign fixtures covered 7,945 pre-choice visits. Baseline keyboard play also completed a 75-visit Face investigation at 390px with paragraph reading and a 28-visit minimal skipped opening at 1440px in text-only mode.

The dry canal had 467 visits but only seven prose variants. The wall arrival had 275 visits and five variants. Distinct jobs and personal answers repeatedly reconverged on the same acknowledgement. In both hubs, optional work preceded the main chapter decision. The audit identifies repetition and navigation cost; fixture lengths and automation seconds do not measure human reading time, fatigue or comprehension.

This cycle changes those returns and makes the chapter decision easier to reach. It adds no scene, rule, choice, reward, route, voice clip or media asset. Initial meetings with Ren and Sera retain their introductions.

## Return writing and continuity

Twenty-seven authored return selectors cover depot delivery/planning, Nia's account or deferral, public source challenges, the archive, Mara's dispatch, coolant repair, rest, clinic notice distribution and wall conversations/filing. The saved last-choice label selects emphasis; earned flags and journal custody supply the facts. Unknown/empty legacy logs retain the neutral hub text. Reading prose changes no state, and native reload preserves the same emphasis.

Private delivery, a broken private promise and agreed registration remain distinct. A courier handover does not imply the runner carried the case. An approved private account grants no public quotation permission. Independent corroboration does not supply a digital-key match. Dispatch, desk receipt, inquiry findings and names spoken remain separate. Ren gains no witness address or private statement; Sera's return grants no forgiveness, cure or unearned closure. The canal still warns that closing the week leaves unfinished commitments open.

## Decision navigation

The hubs separate “People and unfinished work” from “Street or silence” or “At the wall.” When optional choices precede the chapter decision, a native link focuses and scrolls to its heading. The next Tab reaches the first available decision. Activating the link spends nothing and selects nothing. All choices remain visible in their original order, with original numerical indices, number shortcuts, disabled requirements and check previews.

The link complements paragraph reading, existing skip navigation and the campaign commitments panel. Text-only play, largest text, reduced motion and keyboard selection retain the same outcomes.

## Grade and next phase

Simulated, source-familiar play remains the primary development proxy. The internal overall grade stays **8.2/10**; more variants or passing checks alone do not establish a higher player-experience grade. Separate production acceptance gates remain open.

Next, contrast resource-poor and failure-heavy complete campaigns with the four prepared builds. Audit affordable recovery, task resumption, open-commitment visibility and the time spent between consequential decisions. Choose the weakest observed stretch, revise it with explicit art/audio decisions if its scenes change, then repeat identical-state comparisons and full keyboard play. Broader campaign balance follows the evidence; a new feature count is not its acceptance criterion.
