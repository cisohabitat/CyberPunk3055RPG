# Fresh-player character and pacing sessions

This is a prepared protocol, not completed independent research. The current provisional grade and six acceptance gates remain open. Recruit and schedule participants separately; no invitations or player uploads are performed by the game or this implementation.

## Two cohorts

As a planning assumption, start with five fresh players in an opening/encounter cohort and five different fresh players in a full-campaign cohort. Include varied narrative-game experience and accessibility preferences. Obtain agreement before observing or recording; use anonymous participant codes and allow withdrawal. Do not supply interpretation, a preferred proposal or an ending to reach.

### Cohort A: opening and character encounter

Start a new run. Ask: “Play as you normally would. Say what you are trying to do if you are comfortable doing so.” Observe whether the player can explain the job, their own reason for taking it and the difference between the recorded hour and present-day conversation. At the Week, give no directions toward a particular optional mission. If the response encounter is reached, observe the first proposal, refusal interpretation, quiet question and dispatch decision. If it is missed, record that; directed encounter sessions can follow separately and must be labeled directed.

After play, ask neutrally:

- What did Mara want? What did Lumen want?
- What happened to the statement? What remains unanswered?
- Which conversation felt like a person speaking? Which felt repetitive or confusing?
- Where did you want to act but feel you could only continue reading?
- What did you expect your character build to change?

### Cohort B: complete campaign

Start through character creation, keep natural dice and allow the player to choose optional content, reading pace, artwork and sound. Do not require all missions. Observe decision pauses, backtracking, voluntary skips, confusing resources and fatigue. After the finale, ask what their choices changed and which relationships felt unresolved. Ask whether the finale scene and recap agreed with their memory of play.

## Recording and decisions

Use `qa/research/character-observations.csv` as a blank local worksheet. Record observed behavior separately from quotations and the reviewer's interpretation. Never infer player consent from completion. Preserve failed/abandoned sessions. Report denominators, participant experience, accessibility setup, build revision, session type and recruiting limitations. Do not turn a small cohort into population percentages or an automatic craft grade.

Prioritize blockers and repeated comprehension failures before more content. Revise the weakest segment, then test with fresh participants. Evaluate narrative, gameplay, audiovisual, accessibility and technical quality through the existing independent rubric; update a gate only when its required evidence actually exists. Directed internal automation is compatibility evidence, not either cohort.
