## What works and what still weakens the campaign

**Opening and personal stakes.** `opening_city` explains the memory trade, then `opening_self` connects the runner's origin and need for work. The neighbor's account of a sold argument gives the trade a human cost before Quill's offer. The story no longer starts with an unexplained extraction assignment. Mara's confession and reason remain attached to one another without granting forgiveness. The Wall finales have a person and a present-tense action before optional factual history.

**Build recognition and consequences.** The Nerve freight sequence asks for an inspected rest point and actual support, then lets the runner choose a safe stop or overreach. The stopped run keeps its committed effort and uses registered recovery; it does not invent a clinic receipt. The scarce run genuinely spends down, rests, resumes and carries without purchased support. The successful harness run retains its owned kit. These are different practical tasks, although broader depth across every optional encounter is still unfinished.

**Source comprehension.** The archive distinguishes a continuing plant operation from a changed evacuation instruction. A request for an issuer is different from corroborating a ledger; drafting is free and proves neither. Testimony distinguishes a source, permission to quote it and the claim it supports. These distinctions are available in the supplied text and preserved by the tested choices. Source knowledge prevents this proxy from claiming that a new player inferred them unaided.

**Remaining reading and navigation cost.** The fixed ten still reach a **1,891px** prose-to-choice-section gap in the enlarged no-art archive and **1,867px** with phone artwork. Testimony reaches **1,735px** and the desktop route exit **1,107px**. The broader harness campaign also visits `act2_workshop`, where the section is **4,375px** below the prose. Its Phase 8 report records the same workshop gap; this is an existing composition, not a Phase 9 regression. The per-kit **Review purchase** buttons already jump to the corresponding canonical purchase without spending money, which mitigates travel. The full comparison catalog remains long.

The [six fresh layout probes](../qa/editorial/campaign-reassessment-layout.json) additionally distinguish the choice section, first following control and actual commit button. At the inspected phone archive, the first following control is View artwork at 282px, while the first canonical task action is 2,069px below the prose. In the workshop, the first following control is Voice transcript at 82px; the raw first purchase button is 4,547px below the prose. Neither View artwork nor a transcript is a task decision. Earlier inspection controls and purchase-review links must also be considered when judging interaction cost. A smaller section gap alone must not become a cosmetic success criterion.

**Art and performance.** Fresh visual inspection of the empty freight ramp and Edda/Kerr listening paintings finds coherent setting and identity, with no depicted hands or invented receipt/safety result. Six actual enlarged public checkpoints pass axe with no violations or horizontal overflow, and retain exact saves. These samples are not a new anatomy audit of all 164 works. Natural voice completion, exact bindings, bed behavior and original fallback remain technically verifiable; this phase adds no independent expressive audition or physical-speaker test. The previous rejected retakes and ASR disagreements stay available.
