## Findings and implemented response

1. **Repeated depot history.** The prepared campaign mounted the depot ledger on 30 visits, including nine checkpoints where the campaign thread already described it. The standalone ledger now belongs only to twenty explicit depot/specialist contexts. Checkpoint/finale threads retain the complete Edda/Kerr firsthand knowledge, deliberate disclosures and responses in one nested review. Hearing and witness scenes keep their own context.
2. **Slow access to ordinary decisions.** The scarce canal had up to eleven choices and 675–760px of context between prose and the ordinary choice section at enlarged 320px. A visible focus-only link now reaches crowded ordinary lists after the prose is fully revealed. It neither commits a choice nor hides costs, unavailable reasons or commitments. Memory workspaces and the separate archive catalog are excluded so their actual source-selection controls are not bypassed.
3. **The defining cost was buried at the ending.** All three runs received a general opening-motive coda; their wage waiver, exposed room or unfinished search appeared only in longer records. One short earned written reflection now connects that recorded cost to the existing finale. It requires both flags and the appropriate journal record, adds no character quote and claims no new finding, treatment, permission or privacy repair.
4. **Duplicate archive status and a closed-week contradiction.** Checkpoint threads now own read-only archive-plan history. Active planning, archive execution, testimony review and technician conversations retain their relevant controls. A paused failed plan after the week closes no longer advertises free recovery. The existing campaign thread already handled that boundary correctly.
5. **Intermittent mix-test precondition.** Phase 6's WebKit retry was an undefined music-bus observation while speech had already started. The application loads voice and score independently. The overlap fixture now waits for actual music/ambience source starts before releasing intercepted automatic dialogue, then keeps the exact original gain, transcript, actor, order, pause, save and Stop assertions. The numeric assertions retain their 5-second limit and the whole case retains 50 seconds. A separate cold-score test holds the actual score response while speech completes, proves choices remain usable and saves unchanged, then verifies the later unducked music target. Production audio is unchanged.
