# Kerr: the price of a doorway

This phase follows the whole-campaign 8.5/10 internal review. Its goal is one complete dramatic encounter with distinct negotiation, deception and withdrawal rhythms, rather than another roll that converges on the same fight.

## Implementation sequence

1. **Lock the encounter.** A voluntary terms conversation at the chapel offers an honest, costly exit, a checked false-clearance bluff, or a safe withdrawal that leaves the hour behind. Reading and backing out before commitment are free. An unsuccessful bluff cannot be retried. Existing combat and ordinary conversation remain available.
2. **Carry the consequence.** Kerr reacts to the actual outcome when the player returns a week later. The player can own a lie without buying forgiveness or erasing its trace. A short ending reflection remembers the cost without claiming treatment, permission or a repaired relationship.
3. **Match the media.** Enumerate every new scene's art treatment. A new neutral chapel conversation painting establishes the quieter encounter without depicting payment, release or injury. Every new spoken Kerr line receives an exact-text, same-cast recording and provenance; existing recordings remain unchanged.
4. **Validate complete alternatives.** Replay negotiated, successful-bluff, exposed-bluff and withdrawal campaigns to original finales, including scarce resources, held-roll saves and imports. Inspect whole-scene/paragraph/text-only play, phone/desktop/enlarged layouts, speech cancellation and actual visible-word/actor alignment. Preserve the historical 164-route contract separately from this explicitly enumerated new cohort.
5. **Publish the verified phase.** Keep README and AGENTS current. Require source, security, balance, the complete four-profile browser matrix and exact-commit Build, then Vercel READY and public feature/media checks. Record release observations separately from the committed candidate snapshot.

## Compatibility and acceptance

New runs created by the game enable the expanded encounter. The character factory's explicit `expandedKerr` option also enables deterministic new-cohort replays. Existing saves and legacy fixture creation retain their original encounter and pending checks. Save version 2, `saint-shard-3055-v1`, 1d10, stat/strain caps and all four original finales remain.

The honest exit costs twenty credits and a Helion exposure point, visibly previewed before commitment; clinic fare does not prove treatment. The bluff is an actual Face check, never real clearance. Retreat removes the original from the player's custody and forfeits that night's job. No approach grants witness quotation, source authentication or a future payment. Admission records responsibility, not restored trust.

The grade stays provisional. Branch counts and passing tests do not establish acting quality or enjoyment; source-familiar proxy play remains the available playtest method.
