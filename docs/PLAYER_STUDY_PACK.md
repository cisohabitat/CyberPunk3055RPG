## Opening cohort — five fresh players

Allow roughly 20–30 minutes per session, including a short interview; record actual time and stopping point. Seek varied narrative-RPG experience and more than one origin, while letting people choose their own character and settings. Unrepresented origins/devices remain unassessed. Someone who has played, read the source, watched a walkthrough or seen spoilers is not fresh for this cohort.

Start a new run in a disposable profile. Let the participant create a runner, use the opening or voluntary skip, hear Quill's offer and try a first encounter. Use natural dice. Do not import a route, preselect a successful build, explain the memory mystery, encourage a particular choice or solve a check. Ask whether think-aloud is welcome; silence is useful evidence too.

Record time to successful creation, disabled-control confusion, help requests, optional dialogue, skips, first action and voluntary stopping. A settings adjustment is not a failure. If assistance is requested, log what was said and when; results after instruction must be labeled assisted. Stop for a serious access/progress issue and capture a redacted reproduction. Do not push someone through a frustrating session to obtain a completion.

At a natural pause, ask these questions without first naming the correct answer:

- “Who are you in this world, and what are you trying to do next?”
- “Why did your runner come to this meeting? What would the work buy for them?”
- “Have you agreed to anything yet? What remains unanswered?”
- “What did your last choice change? What do you expect to happen?”
- “What made you want to continue, pause or stop?”

After the offer/acceptance, the facilitator can assess target, deadline, payment and opposition using the existing protocol. Before acceptance, understanding that the offer is still unaccepted is a correct answer. Do not mark an unreached job question as an incorrect recollection. Record spontaneous explanations before follow-up prompts; keep those results separate.

## Campaign cohort — five different fresh players

Use five people who did not take the opening study. Start from creation without a walkthrough, fixtures or a required finale. Let players choose optional work and natural dice. Allow breaks and continuation on the same tested build; record actual active time, breaks and any release change. A person may stop or abandon. Report completions and abandonments together, with reasons and unreached questions left unassessed.

Observe at memory reconstruction, a chosen Week encounter and the finale or stopping point. Ask for an explanation before showing factual recaps. Then allow the participant to use the game's journal, acquired sources and aftermath normally. Record whether those tools resolve the confusion.

Use the questions in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) for signature versus later order, recording versus present speech, Nia's actual permissions, observed replies versus promised help and remembered consequences. Record spontaneous build recognition during play and unprompted reflection before mentioning builds, stats or origins. Then ask “What about your runner mattered?” and, if needed, “Did your origin, skill or equipment matter?” Responses to those questions are prompted, even when the question is open. Ask agency on 1–5 and why; log fatigue, repeated decisions, skipped text and where they occurred. Do not infer agency from a successful die or finale.

Compare viable strategies only after blind play: ask which different approach the person would try next. If a directed comparison is useful, label it as a separate directed task. It cannot replace a fresh natural campaign or raise the blind completion count.

## Listening review — all fourteen clips and sustained mix

Use the [clip inventory](../qa/research/listening-clips.csv) to avoid missing a quotation. Its rows identify assets; they contain no listening scores. Human records go in the blank listening sheet. Use the offline [media audition](../qa/media/review.html) for solo files and the actual game for contextual mix. The 72-second preview samples only eight quotations and short beds; it is not the complete fourteen-clip or fatigue review. Asset existence and automated ended cleanup do not establish audible quality.

For each reviewer, record release, date, actual output device, quiet/noisy conditions, volume and game mixer settings. For the first listen, open the inventory's delivered WAV directly, without opening the transcript or audition catalog; note uncertain words before reading the quotation. Then use the transcript source and explicit clip id to compare, followed by the catalog and contextual game mix. Assess intelligibility, delivery, pronunciation, character distinction, clipping/noise and whether music/ambience mask speech. Rate intelligibility and delivery separately on 1–5 with a short rationale. Leave an unassessed rating empty.

Review at least headphones and ordinary speakers, and include a named phone speaker when available. In the game, compare the default mix with the reviewer's preferred mix, explicit replay, Stop and return from real tab/app switching. Report a need to lower beds as a finding rather than silently treating the adjusted mix as default success. Never turn discomfort into a requirement to listen louder.

Listen to reading, quiet and pressure contexts for at least ten actual minutes each where feasible, with breaks. Record actual duration, repeated loop distraction and fatigue; a short audition does not substitute for sustained listening. A failed intelligibility or delivery assessment becomes a per-clip direction/edit task and a same-output retest. Human fatigue and native memory remain separate from the completed cloud endurance probes.

## Device and manual access review

Record physical model, OS/browser version, input, assistive technology, network and preferences in the existing device sheet. Label emulated viewports and internal automation separately. Target named Android/Chrome, iPhone/Safari and desktop setups; manual NVDA/Firefox and VoiceOver/Safari; keyboard-only input and a physical controller when those reviewers are available. Availability is not a completed result or a blanket compatibility claim.

For each supported profile, perform creation → opening → a decision → save/reload → continued decision → factual review. Exercise largest text/high contrast, paragraph and whole-scene reading, silent/text-only play, source disclosures, sheet tabs, Settings and Saves. Verify reading order, labels/odds/cost descriptions, focus after reveal, native-dialog isolation, Escape and opener focus, and access without number shortcuts. Record actual announcements instead of relying on an automated accessibility scan.

On actual devices, interrupt a recorded pending check with app/tab switching and reload; confirm the same die and one committed outcome. Enable sound deliberately, leave the app/tab, return and explicitly resume: no old line or entry cue should replay, and progress should be intact. Check a cold load, warm return and constrained network separately; record timings as samples, not population percentiles. If doing a long device session, record actual minutes and native/resident-memory evidence where available. Do not relabel the cloud two-hour JS measurements as a phone result.
