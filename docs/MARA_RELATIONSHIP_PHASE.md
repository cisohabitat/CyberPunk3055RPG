# Mara: a conversation with a past

This follows the published Kerr encounter (`4808605`) and the provisional 8.5/10 internal review. It deepens the existing present-day Mara arc rather than adding a second trust meter or another dispatch contract.

## Review and scope

Mara already has a voluntary private statement, refusals of public release, company choices, dispatch costs and delayed desk receipt. Those are distinct and remain. The missing beat is a personal response to the runner's own handling of her recorded hour. She must learn that outcome through an actual disclosure; reading a scene cannot give her private player knowledge.

## Acceptance boundaries

The cost here is relational: deflection closes the new personal question, even after acknowledgment. It is visible before commitment and is not converted into an arbitrary money or Strain fee. Declining a personal account is a valid boundary, not a disguised lie. A runner whose night was edited cannot invent a remembered account. Present-day speech never rewrites the recorded confession, source authentication, witness consent, Mara's signature or any inquiry finding.

Preserve version 2, `saint-shard-3055-v1`, saved dice, whole-scene defaults, manual/muted speech, text-only play, 1d10/caps and all four original finales. Source-familiar AI/automation remains the available playtest proxy; technical checks do not establish acting quality, independent player acceptance or a new 9.0/AAA grade.
