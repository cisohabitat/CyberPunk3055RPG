# Saint Shard — participant brief

You are trying a solo story RPG. Play in the way that interests you: choose your runner, read at your own pace and make the choices you prefer. There is no required build or ending, and this is a review of the game, not a test of your ability.

The game includes corporate violence, coercion, grief and memory editing. You may pause, skip optional content or stop at any time. Tell the facilitator if you want to end the session or withdraw your responses under the agreed study policy.

Before beginning, the facilitator will explain the study's purpose, who can see notes, the retention/deletion date and how to withdraw. Observation and recording are separate choices. Agree only to what you are comfortable with. Ask about anything unclear before you start.

Use the tested game URL supplied by the facilitator. Begin a new run in the designated test profile; do not replace progress in your normal player profile. You do not need a player account, real name or exported save. The game stores progress on the device/browser used for play.

Settings let you adjust reading, text size, contrast, motion, artwork and optional sound. You may change these whenever you want. Sound is optional. Ask for help if access or comfort makes playing difficult; the facilitator will record assistance so the results remain fair.

Make your own choices and use the game's dice normally. You do not need a walkthrough, source files, an ending catalog or another player's report. If you already know this game or have seen spoilers, tell the facilitator so your session can be classified correctly.

Thinking aloud is optional if agreed as part of your session. The facilitator may ask what you think is happening at a natural pause and how decisions felt. There is no need to guess what answer they want. Confusion, disagreement and stopping are useful feedback.

Opening sessions are planned for roughly 20–30 minutes including conversation. Full-campaign sessions may take longer and can include breaks; the facilitator will agree scheduling with you. Neither session requires you to finish if you want to stop.
